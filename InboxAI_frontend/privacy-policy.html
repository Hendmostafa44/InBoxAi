Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 12, 2026

Welcome to MailPilot.

MailPilot is an AI-powered email management application that helps users organize, understand, and manage their Gmail emails. This Privacy Policy explains what information MailPilot collects, how it is used, and how it is protected.

1. Information We Collect

When you use MailPilot, we may collect:

Google Account Information

When you sign in with Google, we may receive:

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • Your Google account identifier

Gmail Information

If you connect your Gmail account and grant permission, MailPilot may access Gmail information necessary to provide its features, including:

  • Email sender and recipient information
  • Email subject
  • Email content
  • Email date and time
  • Gmail message identifiers

MailPilot may also use Gmail permissions to send emails when you explicitly request this through the application.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we access to provide and improve MailPilot features, including:

  • Signing you into your MailPilot account
  • Synchronizing your Gmail emails
  • Organizing and displaying emails
  • Generating AI-powered email summaries
  • Identifying tasks, deadlines, and priorities
  • Helping you manage tasks
  • Sending emails through Gmail when you explicitly request it
  • Maintaining and improving the security and functionality of the application

We do not sell your personal information or Gmail data to third parties.

3. AI Processing

MailPilot uses artificial intelligence to analyze email content and generate summaries, tasks, deadlines, and priorities.

Email content may be processed by third-party AI service providers used by MailPilot to provide these features.

We only use such processing to provide the requested MailPilot functionality.

4. Gmail API Data

MailPilot's use of information received from Google APIs complies with Google's API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

MailPilot only requests Gmail permissions that are necessary for its features.

MailPilot does not use Gmail data for advertising purposes.

MailPilot does not sell Gmail data.

5. Data Storage

Information used by MailPilot may be stored in our database and application infrastructure.

We take reasonable technical measures to protect stored information from unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure.

Authentication credentials and OAuth tokens are handled by the application backend and are not intentionally exposed to the application's frontend.

6. Data Sharing

MailPilot does not sell or rent your personal information.

We may use third-party service providers that help operate the application, such as:

  • Database infrastructure providers
  • Cloud hosting providers
  • AI service providers
  • Google services required for Gmail integration

These providers process information only as necessary to provide their services.

7. Gmail Permissions

MailPilot requests Gmail permissions only when necessary for Gmail-related features.

You can revoke MailPilot's access to your Google Account at any time through your Google Account security settings.

Revoking Gmail permissions may prevent Gmail-related MailPilot features from working.

8. Data Deletion

You may request deletion of your MailPilot data.

Deleting your MailPilot account or requesting deletion may remove information associated with your account from our systems, subject to technical, legal, or security requirements.

You may also revoke MailPilot's access to Gmail through your Google Account settings.

9. Children's Privacy

MailPilot is not intended for children under the age of 13.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time.

When changes are made, the updated version will be published on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
